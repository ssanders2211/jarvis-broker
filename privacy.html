Privacy policy

jarvis-broker is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, with the owner's own Google account.

  • Data accessed: the owner's Gmail, Google Drive and Google Calendar, through the scopes shown on the consent screen.
  • Use: to read, label and organize the owner's own data for the owner. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • Storage: OAuth tokens are kept in Windows Credential Manager on the owner's computer. Google data is not sent to any server run by this app; there is none.
  • Transfers: none to third parties.
  • Revoking access: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
